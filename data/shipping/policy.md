# Shipping policy

P1. Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days within the country.
P2. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 12 dollars.
P3. Orders over 50 dollars ship free with standard delivery.
P4. A tracking link is emailed within 24 hours of dispatch.
P5. International orders take 7 to 14 business days and customers pay customs duties.
P6. A parcel not delivered 10 business days after dispatch is treated as lost and is reshipped or refunded.
P7. Delivery address changes are possible only before the order is dispatched.
P8. We do not ship to PO boxes.
