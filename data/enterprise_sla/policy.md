# Enterprise Service Levels policy

P1. Enterprise plans carry a 99.9 percent monthly uptime commitment.
P2. Uptime below 99.9 percent earns a service credit of 10 percent of the monthly fee.
P3. Uptime below 99.0 percent earns a service credit of 25 percent of the monthly fee.
P4. Service credits must be requested within 30 days of the end of the affected month.
P5. Severity 1 incidents receive a first response within 30 minutes, 24 hours a day.
P6. Planned maintenance windows are announced 5 days in advance and are excluded from uptime.
P7. Each enterprise account has a named technical account manager.
P8. Service credits are applied to the next invoice and are not paid out in cash.
