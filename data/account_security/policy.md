# Account Security policy

P1. Two factor authentication can be enabled in Settings, then Security, using an authenticator app or SMS.
P2. After 5 failed login attempts the account is locked for 30 minutes.
P3. Account recovery requires a verified email address and takes up to 24 hours to complete.
P4. Support will never ask for your password or full card number.
P5. Suspicious login alerts are sent to the primary email within 5 minutes of the event.
P6. Recovery codes are shown once at setup and cannot be shown again.
P7. Sessions on other devices can be ended from Settings, then Security, then Active sessions.
P8. Accounts reported as compromised are frozen until the owner passes identity verification.
