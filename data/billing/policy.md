# Billing policy

P1. Invoices are issued on the 1st of each month and are due within 14 days.
P2. Payments received after 14 days incur a late fee of 2 percent of the outstanding balance.
P3. A customer can change the billing date once every 12 months.
P4. Invoice copies for the past 24 months can be downloaded from Settings, then Billing.
P5. Duplicate charges are refunded to the original card within 5 business days of confirmation.
P6. A tax ID can be added before an invoice is issued, but issued invoices cannot be edited.
P7. Payments are accepted by card, bank transfer and direct debit. Cash is not accepted.
P8. Billing disputes must be raised within 60 days of the invoice date.
