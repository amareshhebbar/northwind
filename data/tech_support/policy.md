# Technical Support policy

P1. The app supports iOS 16 and later and Android 10 and later.
P2. Password reset links expire after 30 minutes.
P3. If the app crashes on launch, update to the latest version and restart the device before contacting support.
P4. Sync problems are fixed by signing out and back in, which re-downloads all data.
P5. Priority 1 issues that block all use of the product get a response within 1 hour on weekdays.
P6. Support hours are 8 am to 8 pm on weekdays and 10 am to 4 pm on Saturdays.
P7. Support does not troubleshoot jailbroken or rooted devices.
P8. Logs can be sent from Settings, then Help, then Send diagnostics.
