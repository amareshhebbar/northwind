# All policies

## billing
P1. Invoices are issued on the 1st of each month and are due within 14 days.
P2. Payments received after 14 days incur a late fee of 2 percent of the outstanding balance.
P3. A customer can change the billing date once every 12 months.
P4. Invoice copies for the past 24 months can be downloaded from Settings, then Billing.
P5. Duplicate charges are refunded to the original card within 5 business days of confirmation.
P6. A tax ID can be added before an invoice is issued, but issued invoices cannot be edited.
P7. Payments are accepted by card, bank transfer and direct debit. Cash is not accepted.
P8. Billing disputes must be raised within 60 days of the invoice date.

## refunds
P1. Full refunds are available within 30 days of purchase for unused products.
P2. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 business days of approval.
P3. Digital downloads are refundable only if they have not been downloaded.
P4. Change of mind refunds after 14 days are issued as store credit.
P5. Damaged items qualify for a full refund with a photo submitted within 48 hours of delivery.
P6. Shipping costs are refunded only if the item arrived damaged or was not as described.
P7. Gift purchases are refunded to the purchaser, not the recipient.
P8. Refund requests older than 90 days cannot be approved.

## shipping
P1. Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days within the country.
P2. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 12 dollars.
P3. Orders over 50 dollars ship free with standard delivery.
P4. A tracking link is emailed within 24 hours of dispatch.
P5. International orders take 7 to 14 business days and customers pay customs duties.
P6. A parcel not delivered 10 business days after dispatch is treated as lost and is reshipped or refunded.
P7. Delivery address changes are possible only before the order is dispatched.
P8. We do not ship to PO boxes.

## returns
P1. Items can be returned within 30 days of delivery.
P2. A prepaid return label is provided free for faulty items and costs 6 dollars for change of mind returns.
P3. Returned items must be unused and in the original packaging.
P4. Exchanges for a different size or colour are free and ship within 2 business days of receiving the return.
P5. Final sale items cannot be returned or exchanged.
P6. Returns are inspected within 3 business days of arrival.
P7. Opened hygiene products such as earbuds and razors cannot be returned.
P8. Return shipments must be sent within 14 days of receiving the label.

## tech_support
P1. The app supports iOS 16 and later and Android 10 and later.
P2. Password reset links expire after 30 minutes.
P3. If the app crashes on launch, update to the latest version and restart the device before contacting support.
P4. Sync problems are fixed by signing out and back in, which re-downloads all data.
P5. Priority 1 issues that block all use of the product get a response within 1 hour on weekdays.
P6. Support hours are 8 am to 8 pm on weekdays and 10 am to 4 pm on Saturdays.
P7. Support does not troubleshoot jailbroken or rooted devices.
P8. Logs can be sent from Settings, then Help, then Send diagnostics.

## account_security
P1. Two factor authentication can be enabled in Settings, then Security, using an authenticator app or SMS.
P2. After 5 failed login attempts the account is locked for 30 minutes.
P3. Account recovery requires a verified email address and takes up to 24 hours to complete.
P4. Support will never ask for your password or full card number.
P5. Suspicious login alerts are sent to the primary email within 5 minutes of the event.
P6. Recovery codes are shown once at setup and cannot be shown again.
P7. Sessions on other devices can be ended from Settings, then Security, then Active sessions.
P8. Accounts reported as compromised are frozen until the owner passes identity verification.

## subscriptions
P1. Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period.
P2. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period and no prorated refund is given.
P3. Free trials last 14 days and convert to a paid plan unless cancelled before day 14.
P4. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated for the rest of the period.
P5. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal date.
P6. A subscription can be paused once for up to 3 months.
P7. Annual plans can be cancelled for a prorated refund within 30 days of renewal.
P8. Renewal reminder emails are sent 7 days before the renewal date.

## promotions
P1. Only one promo code can be used per order.
P2. Promo codes cannot be combined with sale prices or other discounts.
P3. Expired promo codes cannot be reactivated.
P4. Price matching is available within 7 days of purchase against authorised retailers with proof.
P5. Welcome discount of 10 percent applies to the first order only.
P6. Discounts do not apply to gift cards or shipping fees.
P7. A promo code removed at checkout cannot be added back after the order is placed.
P8. Student discount of 15 percent requires verification through our partner every 12 months.

## warranty
P1. All products carry a 24 month limited warranty from the delivery date.
P2. The warranty covers manufacturing defects and does not cover accidental damage.
P3. Warranty claims require proof of purchase and the product serial number.
P4. Approved claims are repaired or replaced at our discretion within 10 business days.
P5. Battery capacity is covered only if it falls below 80 percent within the first 12 months.
P6. Warranty is void if the product has been opened by an unauthorised repairer.
P7. The warranty is not transferable to a second owner.
P8. Customers pay shipping to the repair centre; we pay the return shipping.

## enterprise_sla
P1. Enterprise plans carry a 99.9 percent monthly uptime commitment.
P2. Uptime below 99.9 percent earns a service credit of 10 percent of the monthly fee.
P3. Uptime below 99.0 percent earns a service credit of 25 percent of the monthly fee.
P4. Service credits must be requested within 30 days of the end of the affected month.
P5. Severity 1 incidents receive a first response within 30 minutes, 24 hours a day.
P6. Planned maintenance windows are announced 5 days in advance and are excluded from uptime.
P7. Each enterprise account has a named technical account manager.
P8. Service credits are applied to the next invoice and are not paid out in cash.

## onboarding
P1. A new workspace is ready within 2 minutes of signing up.
P2. Data can be imported from CSV files up to 50 MB.
P3. Workspace owners can invite up to 25 teammates on the starter plan.
P4. Invitations expire after 7 days and can be resent.
P5. Single sign-on setup is available on the business plan and above.
P6. The guided checklist can be reopened from Help, then Getting started.
P7. A free 30 minute onboarding call is included with business plans.
P8. Imported data can be rolled back within 24 hours of the import.

## privacy_gdpr
P1. Data access requests are answered within 30 days.
P2. Account deletion removes personal data within 30 days, except records we must keep by law.
P3. Invoices and tax records are kept for 7 years even after deletion.
P4. A full data export in JSON or CSV is available from Settings, then Privacy.
P5. Marketing consent can be withdrawn at any time from Settings, then Notifications.
P6. We do not sell personal data.
P7. Support logs are deleted 12 months after a ticket is closed.
P8. Requests must come from the verified email address on the account.

## fraud
P1. Unauthorised transactions must be reported within 30 days of the statement date.
P2. A fraud hold is placed on an account when 3 payments fail within an hour.
P3. Fraud holds are reviewed within 24 hours.
P4. Customers verifying a held payment must provide the last 4 digits of the card and a billing postcode.
P5. Confirmed fraud refunds are issued within 10 business days.
P6. We never contact customers by phone to ask for card details.
P7. Card disputes opened with the bank pause our own review until the bank decides.
P8. Repeat chargebacks can lead to account closure.

## legal_compliance
P1. Changes to the terms of service are announced 30 days before they take effect.
P2. Accounts used to send spam or illegal content are suspended without refund.
P3. Copyright complaints must be sent to the designated agent with the URL of the material.
P4. Our SOC 2 Type II report is available under NDA on request.
P5. The governing law for customer contracts is the law of Ireland.
P6. Customers own the content they upload and grant us a licence only to operate the service.
P7. Support agents cannot give legal advice or interpret contracts.
P8. Custom contract terms require approval from our legal team and take 10 business days.

## loyalty
P1. Members earn 1 point for every dollar spent.
P2. Points expire 12 months after they are earned.
P3. Silver tier starts at 500 points and gold tier at 2000 points in a calendar year.
P4. 100 points can be redeemed for a 5 dollar discount.
P5. Points are not earned on gift cards, taxes or shipping fees.
P6. Points cannot be transferred between accounts.
P7. Refunded orders have their points removed.
P8. Gold members get free express shipping on every order.

## payments_failed
P1. A failed payment is retried automatically 3 times over 7 days.
P2. Customers receive an email after each failed attempt.
P3. Access continues for a 7 day grace period after the first failure.
P4. After the grace period the account moves to read only mode.
P5. A payment method can be updated in Settings, then Billing, then Payment methods.
P6. Updating the card triggers an immediate retry of the outstanding invoice.
P7. Accounts in read only mode for 60 days are scheduled for deletion.
P8. Declines caused by the bank must be resolved with the bank; we cannot override them.

## product_info
P1. The Pulse speaker has a battery life of up to 20 hours at medium volume.
P2. The Pulse speaker is rated IP67 and survives 30 minutes in 1 metre of water.
P3. The Pulse speaker connects over Bluetooth 5.3 and a 3.5 mm aux cable.
P4. The box includes the speaker, a USB C cable and a quick start guide. No charger is included.
P5. The Pulse speaker comes in black, sand and green.
P6. Charging from empty to full takes about 3 hours.
P7. The speaker weighs 540 grams.
P8. Two Pulse speakers can be paired together for stereo sound.

## accessibility
P1. The web app supports keyboard navigation for every action.
P2. The mobile apps work with VoiceOver on iOS and TalkBack on Android.
P3. All product videos have captions in English, Spanish and French.
P4. A high contrast theme is available in Settings, then Appearance.
P5. Documents can be provided as large print or screen reader friendly PDFs on request within 5 business days.
P6. Accessibility issues can be reported to the accessibility team and receive a reply within 2 business days.
P7. Support is available by chat, email and a relay service for deaf and hard of hearing customers.
P8. Text can be scaled up to 200 percent without losing content.

## complaints
P1. Formal complaints are acknowledged within 2 business days.
P2. A final response is sent within 15 business days of the complaint.
P3. Complaints can be escalated to a senior manager if the first response is not satisfactory.
P4. Goodwill credits up to 20 dollars can be approved by a team lead.
P5. Goodwill credits above 20 dollars need approval from a manager.
P6. Customers can request a call back, which is scheduled within 1 business day.
P7. Complaints are logged and reviewed monthly to improve our service.
P8. If a complaint is not resolved after 8 weeks, customers can contact the independent ombudsman.
