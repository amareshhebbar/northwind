# Onboarding policy

P1. A new workspace is ready within 2 minutes of signing up.
P2. Data can be imported from CSV files up to 50 MB.
P3. Workspace owners can invite up to 25 teammates on the starter plan.
P4. Invitations expire after 7 days and can be resent.
P5. Single sign-on setup is available on the business plan and above.
P6. The guided checklist can be reopened from Help, then Getting started.
P7. A free 30 minute onboarding call is included with business plans.
P8. Imported data can be rolled back within 24 hours of the import.
