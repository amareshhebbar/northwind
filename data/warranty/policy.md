# Warranty policy

P1. All products carry a 24 month limited warranty from the delivery date.
P2. The warranty covers manufacturing defects and does not cover accidental damage.
P3. Warranty claims require proof of purchase and the product serial number.
P4. Approved claims are repaired or replaced at our discretion within 10 business days.
P5. Battery capacity is covered only if it falls below 80 percent within the first 12 months.
P6. Warranty is void if the product has been opened by an unauthorised repairer.
P7. The warranty is not transferable to a second owner.
P8. Customers pay shipping to the repair centre; we pay the return shipping.
