# Subscriptions policy

P1. Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period.
P2. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period and no prorated refund is given.
P3. Free trials last 14 days and convert to a paid plan unless cancelled before day 14.
P4. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated for the rest of the period.
P5. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal date.
P6. A subscription can be paused once for up to 3 months.
P7. Annual plans can be cancelled for a prorated refund within 30 days of renewal.
P8. Renewal reminder emails are sent 7 days before the renewal date.
