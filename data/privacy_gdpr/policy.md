# Privacy And Data Requests policy

P1. Data access requests are answered within 30 days.
P2. Account deletion removes personal data within 30 days, except records we must keep by law.
P3. Invoices and tax records are kept for 7 years even after deletion.
P4. A full data export in JSON or CSV is available from Settings, then Privacy.
P5. Marketing consent can be withdrawn at any time from Settings, then Notifications.
P6. We do not sell personal data.
P7. Support logs are deleted 12 months after a ticket is closed.
P8. Requests must come from the verified email address on the account.
