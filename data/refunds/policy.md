# Refunds policy

P1. Full refunds are available within 30 days of purchase for unused products.
P2. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 business days of approval.
P3. Digital downloads are refundable only if they have not been downloaded.
P4. Change of mind refunds after 14 days are issued as store credit.
P5. Damaged items qualify for a full refund with a photo submitted within 48 hours of delivery.
P6. Shipping costs are refunded only if the item arrived damaged or was not as described.
P7. Gift purchases are refunded to the purchaser, not the recipient.
P8. Refund requests older than 90 days cannot be approved.
